# NoteWorthy — Engagement & Gamification

The goal is an app people *want* to open, built on competence rather than compulsion. Everything below is designed to survive the question: **"would a serious musician find this respectful?"**

---

## 1. The engagement thesis

The strongest hook we have is not points — it's **"I can hear things I couldn't hear last month."** Every mechanic exists to make that progress *visible, frequent, and credible*. Points are the wrapper; perceived competence is the product.

Three loops, at three timescales:

| Loop | Duration | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | 2–5 seconds | Item → answer → musical feedback → next. Must feel instant and satisfying. |
| **Session** | 5–10 minutes | Warm-Up → combo/accuracy → session summary with one specific observation. |
| **Meta** | days–months | Map unlocks, mastery levels, Musicianship Profile, Practice Journal, Key of the Week. |

If the micro loop isn't fun on its own, no amount of meta-layer will save it. **Build and tune the micro loop first.**

---

## 2. Musical feedback design ⭐

The single most distinctive engagement choice: **the reward layer is made of music, in the current key.**

| Event | Sound |
|---|---|
| Correct | The answered note resolving to tonic, or a soft I-chord ping — always diatonic, always in the current key |
| Correct + combo | The confirmation ascends the scale as the combo builds (3rd → 5th → octave), so a hot streak literally sounds like a rising arpeggio |
| Wrong | Not a buzzer. A brief, soft dissonance, then immediately the **A/B comparison**: your answer, the correct answer, correct-in-context |
| Level up | A short authentic cadence stinger in the week's key |
| Streak milestone | A fuller voicing of the same stinger |
| Session complete | A resolved cadence — closure, literally |

Every sound is generated by the same audio engine as the drills, in the current key and tempo. Consequences: the app always sounds coherent, users get thousands of extra incidental exposures to tonal function, and it feels expensive.

**Visual feedback** is restrained to match: a pulse of accent color, subtle scale, a particle wash only on genuinely rare events. No confetti cannons, no cartoon mascots.

## 3. Progression systems

### 3.1 XP
- Awarded per item, scaled by **difficulty × correctness × modality**.
- **Production modalities pay more**: singing an answer > notating it > selecting it. This nudges people toward the drills that teach best.
- Combo multiplier within a session (×1.0 → ×2.0, resets on error) — rewards focus, resets cheaply.
- **No XP decay, ever.** Punishing absence is a dark pattern and drives churn.

### 3.2 Levels
Levels are cosmetic-plus: each unlocks a real thing (a new drill mode, an instrument timbre, a Map region, a theme). Level names use musical terms — *Novice → Apprentice → Journeyman → Artisan → Virtuoso → Maestro*, subdivided.

### 3.3 The Musicianship Map ⭐
The primary progression surface. A constellation of skill nodes across all four pillars, with visible prerequisite edges.

- Nodes show mastery state: locked / available / practicing / proficient / mastered / needs review.
- **"Needs review" nodes visibly dim over time** as FSRS retention decays — a beautiful, honest signal that creates genuine pull back to the app without a single guilt notification.
- Regions correspond to musical domains (Rhythm, Melody, Harmony, Reading, Voice), and they interconnect — the Map *shows* that harmony hearing depends on triad theory.
- Zoomable, pannable, gorgeous. This is the screen people screenshot.

### 3.4 Musicianship Profile
A six-axis radar: **Pitch · Rhythm · Harmony · Reading · Voice · Theory**. Updated continuously; snapshotted monthly so you can see the shape change over time. Gives users an identity ("I'm strong on harmony, weak on rhythm") that a single XP number never can.

### 3.5 Streaks — supportive, not coercive
- A streak advances by completing the **Daily Warm-Up** (~5 min) — an honest, achievable bar.
- **Fermata** ♩𝄐: automatic streak-hold days, earned by practicing (1 per 7 active days, max 3 banked). Applied silently, no purchase, no ask.
- **Streak repair** is free and always available within 48 hours.
- Notifications are gentle and capped at one per day, and the settings offer "never" without friction.
- **We never say "you're about to lose your streak."** The copy is "your ear is getting rusty on X" — true, useful, and it points at practice rather than at anxiety.

### 3.6 Badges
Specific and earned, never participation trophies:
- *Perfect Fifth* — 50 consecutive correct P5 identifications
- *Golden Ear* — 95%+ on a full functional degree set with no context
- *Sight Reader* — 20 real repertoire excerpts at first sight
- *In Tune* — a full agility run inside ±10¢
- *Full Circle* — complete all five steps with 90%+ on every one
- *Round the Circle* — a full 12-week circle-of-fifths cycle

### 3.7 Key of the Week ⭐
Every Monday the tonal center rotates around the circle of fifths. All drills, warm-ups, and reading material use it. Effects: genuine per-key familiarity, a natural weekly rhythm, and a 12-week macro-cycle worth completing.

## 4. Session design

### 4.1 The summary screen
After every session, exactly **one specific observation** — never a wall of stats:

> "Your descending 6ths are 30% slower than your ascending ones. Worth a few minutes."

Plus: accuracy, XP, one node that moved, and the next-session teaser. Specific > comprehensive.

### 4.2 Flow-state protection
- No interruptions mid-drill. Achievements queue and fire *between* items, never during.
- Difficulty auto-tunes to ~82% success — the empirical sweet spot for flow and learning alike.
- One-tap "keep going" at the end; the friction to continue must be near zero.

### 4.3 Practice modes for different moods
- **Warm-Up** — the daily 5-minute assembled session
- **Focus** — grind one concept (blocked practice, user's choice)
- **Marathon** — endless mode with escalating difficulty and a personal best
- **Recital** — the boss battle, 3 lives, opt-in
- **Zen** — no scoring, no timer, no XP; just the drill. Some days people want to practice without being measured, and offering this *increases* trust in the rest of the system.

---

## 5. Anti-patterns — explicitly forbidden

| Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Energy/hearts that block practice | Blocking someone from learning to monetize is indefensible, and we're free anyway |
| Streak-loss guilt notifications | Manufactured anxiety; drives long-term churn |
| XP decay | Punishes life happening |
| Ads | Breaks focus and the premium feel outright |
| Fake urgency / countdown FOMO | Dishonest |
| Leaderboards as the primary loop | Turns learning into comparison; demotivates the median user |
| Confetti for trivial actions | Cheapens the real milestones |
| Locking core content behind anything | Everything is free, period |

---

## 6. What we measure

**Learning outcomes** (do we actually work?)
- Accuracy at fixed difficulty over time, per concept
- Time-to-mastery per node
- Retention: accuracy on items due after ≥7 days

**Engagement** (do people come back?)
- D1 / D7 / D30 retention, sessions/week, session length
- Warm-Up completion rate
- Drop-off point within each drill type

**Health** (are the mechanics honest?)
- Ratio of Zen-mode to scored sessions (high = scoring feels stressful)
- Streak-repair usage (high = the bar is set wrong)
- Notification opt-out rate (high = we're being annoying)

All analytics are local-first and anonymous, with a clear opt-out. An app about honest self-assessment shouldn't be shady about data.
